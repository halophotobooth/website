How it works

On the night

We arrive early, set up and run the booth the whole time, so you don't have to think about it.

Arrive

Guests walk up to the booth, grab a prop from the box and step in front of the backdrop.

Pose

Tap the screen, and the countdown takes three shots with flattering studio lighting.

Print

Their strip prints with your event's design in about 15 seconds, ready to take home.

Share

They scan a QR code to get the digital copies on their phone, and you get the full gallery after.

Questions

Good to know

How much does it cost?

Half day (up to 3 hours) is €249 and full day (up to 6 hours) is €449 at our founding rate. See packages for what's included. Send us your date and we'll confirm within a day.

Can we see photos from past events?

Not yet. We're just starting out, so the samples on this page are designs with illustrated stand-ins. Our first events are in January 2027 and real photos will go up here and on Instagram straight after.

How much space does the booth need?

About 2m by 2m near a power socket. We bring everything else, including the backdrop and lighting.

How do guests get their digital copies?

After the event we share a link to every photo from the night so guests can download their own.

How are the 3 free societies picked?

First come, first served, as long as the date works for us. Once the three spots are gone, they're gone.

How far ahead should we book?

Ball season fills up fast, especially February and March. Get in touch as soon as you have a date.
