Questions

Good to know

How much does it cost?

It depends on the length of the event, the number of prints and travel. Send us your date and we'll come back with a price within a day.

How much space does the booth need?

About 2m by 2m near a power socket. We bring everything else, including the backdrop and lighting.

How do guests get their digital copies?

After the event we share a link to every photo from the night so guests can download their own.

How are the 3 free societies picked?

First come, first served, as long as the date works for us. Once the three spots are gone, they're gone.

How far ahead should we book?

Ball season fills up fast, especially February and March. Get in touch as soon as you have a date.
